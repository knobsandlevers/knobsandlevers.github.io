About

Crafting Sound
That Tells Stories

I'm Blake Giancoli — a composer, musician, and sound designer whose work lives at the edge of electronic synthesis and cinematic texture. I've spent years building a catalog that spans moody synth-driven albums, experimental sound design, and original scores for independent film.

My goal is simple: score more video games and films. I bring a deep understanding of interactive audio, adaptive music systems, and the kind of emotional specificity that makes a scene land. Whether you need a sweeping orchestral moment or a micro-tension sound bed, I'm built for it.

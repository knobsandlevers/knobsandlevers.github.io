First Concepts — ECE Topics #1

Started MegawattKW's ECE Topics series this morning. Episode 1 covers the four fundamentals: voltage, current, resistance, and power.

V = IR  |  P = VI  |  P = I²R

Real-World Scale

  • Red LED — ~2V, a few milliamps, about 9mW
  • USB — 5V, 2A, 10W
  • Electric Vehicle — 800V, 200A, 160kW
The same Ohm's Law that governs a tiny LED scales up to 215 horsepower in an EV. Wild.
